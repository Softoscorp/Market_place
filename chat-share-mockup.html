Sending an Apartment in Chat — No Copy-Pasting Links

To clear this up: you never copy a link and paste it. Instead, the apartment is sent as a card message — like sending a photo, but it's the listing. The sender taps Share → picks an existing conversation, and the receiver sees a real listing card pop up inside the chat. Tapping the card opens the full property page. Below: how it works, then desktop chat, the share picker, and the phone version.

Static mockup — review the flow, then I'll build it into the chat.

How sharing works (no links involved)
It is not a copy-paste link. The apartment is sent as a rich card message, exactly like sending an image. Three taps:
STEP 1
Open the listing
On any apartment page or card, tap the Share button.
STEP 2
Pick a conversation
A sheet slides up listing your existing chats. Pick one person.
STEP 3
Card appears in chat
The listing card pops into the chat. The other person taps it to open the page.
