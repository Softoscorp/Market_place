🌍 International Booking — Middleman Flow PLANNED FOR LATER
Separate from local messaging. For students abroad who want to secure an apartment before landing on the island. The platform acts as the trusted middleman: the renter pays into a platform wallet, we confirm funds, then we coordinate with the specific agent who posted the apartment. No money is paid directly to the agent — it goes through us.
Step 1
Student abroad picks an apartment
Sees any listing on the site and taps "Book before arrival" — marked with the 🌍 international badge.
Step 2
Pays into platform wallet
Sends the deposit / first payment to our platform wallet (international transfer). We confirm the funds arrive.
Step 3
We contact the agent
The platform routes the booking to the agent who posted it — verified local or international — with all renter details.
Step 4
Held & settled on arrival
Payment stays protected in the wallet until the student arrives and moves in. Platform service fee deducted (amount TBD later).
International badge tie-in: agents who hold the 🌍 international badge get flagged as "prefers international bookings" and appear first for abroad renters. The platform fee model (per-booking fee vs. percentage vs. referral) is not decided yet — we'll pick that later.
Note: no payment gateway is connected yet. When we do add one (Stripe / PayPal / both — TBD), the wallet gets a real payment card option for Step 2. For now this whole flow stays as a plan.
